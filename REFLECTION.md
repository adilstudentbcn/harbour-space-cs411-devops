Challenge 5 - Reflection

The toughest part of this challenge was getting used to the AWS console while trying to fix my pipeline when it kept failing. Moving from the simple "playground" we used before to a real cloud environment was a big jump, and it was pretty frustrating when builds kept breaking because I’d missed one small setting.

At one point, the AI started suggesting some really complicated and irrelevant ways to lock down my security groups. That forced me to stop and actually think through what the AI was saying instead of just copy-pasting its code. It ended up being a great lesson because it forced me to really learn how network rules work.

If I could do this again, I’d definitely make a clear, step-by-step checklist with screenshots for every single step. Having a guide like that would make sure I don't forget the little things—like opening the right ports or getting the permissions just right—which would make my future deployments way less stressful.
